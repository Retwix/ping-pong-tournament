# Plan: Les Anciens — archiving players who left the company

**Branch**: feat/alumni-anciens
**Status**: Active
**Spec**: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-13-alumni-design.md`

## Goal

A player who has left the company stops occupying a spot in the live ladder and
the default player picker, while keeping every match, record and season title
they earned.

## Global constraints

**Archiving is a display filter, never a data change.** Ratings are replayed from
match history on every load (`rating.ts:replayRatings`, `db.ts:291`). An
alumnus's matches must stay inside `ratedMatches`. If they were dropped, every
opponent they ever played would be re-rated, the whole ladder would shift, and a
closed season could acquire a different champion. No slice below removes a match
from the replay, and no slice writes to `rating_events`.

**This project has no component tests.** There is no Testing Library or Vitest
Browser Mode in `package.json` — every test in `src/lib/*.test.ts` is a pure
function test, and Thibault verifies visuals themself rather than through
automated browser checks. That shapes the slices: all decision logic goes into
pure modules under `src/lib/` where a failing test can be written first, and the
components stay thin and declarative. **A slice is not allowed to put a rule
inside a component.** If a slice finds itself wanting to branch inside JSX, that
branch belongs in a tested selector.

**Testing conventions** (from the existing suite): Vitest, `describe`/`it`,
factory helpers built inline per test file (see `seasons.test.ts:26` `at()` and
`:358` the player factory), no `let`/`beforeEach`, real types from `src/types.ts`
never redefined. French domain vocabulary in user-facing strings, English in
code.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A player can be marked as having left the company, with a departure date, from the Joueurs annuaire
- [x] An archived player appears with a black-and-white avatar wherever they appear as themselves (annuaire + all-time ladder; no other surfaces exist yet)
- [x] An archived player holds no rank in « Le classement » — active players fill ranks 1..N with no holes (all-time and season scopes, per the closure rule)
- [x] An archived player who played in the current scope is listed under « Anciens » below the ladder, with their frozen rating and departure date, and no rank number
- [x] An archived player never appears in the podium, the tightest-gaps pill, the top-progressions card, or the dashboard « Top joueurs »
- [x] An archived player is not ranked in any season that closed after they left, including every new season
- [x] An archived player keeps their title in a season that closed before they left
- [ ] An archived player is hidden from the Nouvelle Partie picker by default and reachable behind a « Voir les anciens » toggle
- [ ] A partie containing an archived player is forced « non classée » and says why
- [x] An archived player keeps every all-time record, head-to-head and stat they earned (Stats page and rating replay untouched by design)
- [x] Un-archiving returns a player to the ladder with their rating intact

## Slices

### Slice 1: Thibault marks a player as having left the company

**Status**: ✅ Implemented and green (2026-08-14) — RED/GREEN/MUTATE(8/8 killed)/REFACTOR all done. Outstanding: the migration still needs to be run against the real Supabase project.

**Value**: Thibault can record the fact of a departure and see it reflected in
the annuaire. Nothing about the ladder changes yet — this slice makes the truth
storable and visible, which every later slice reads.

**Path**: Joueurs annuaire → open a player's edit modal → toggle « A quitté
l'entreprise » → date field prefills with today → save → `updatePlayer` persists
`status` + `left_at` → realtime refresh → the row returns muted, black-and-white,
sorted last, behind a new « Anciens » filter chip.

Skipped intentionally: the ladder still ranks them (slice 2), the picker still
offers them (slice 4).

**Required implementation skills**: `tdd`, `testing`, `mutation-testing`,
`refactoring`. Plus `typescript-strict` for the status union at the DB boundary.

**Acceptance criteria**:
- Marking a player as departed persists their status and departure date
- A departed player sorts below every active player in the annuaire regardless of Elo
- A departed player's row is muted and their avatar renders in black and white
- The annuaire hides departed players by default; an « Anciens » chip reveals them
- The chip shows a count, and does not render when nobody has left
- Un-toggling restores the player to the active list and clears the departure date

**RED**: `src/lib/joueurs.test.ts` — extend the existing suite.
- `joueurRows` carries `status` and `leftAt` through from the registry, and sorts alumni last regardless of rating: an alumnus on 1700 comes after an active player on 1200
- `filterJoueurs` excludes alumni unless the alumni filter is on
- a new `departureLabel(leftAt)` returns « parti·e en juin 2026 » from a date, and null for a missing one
- `teamChips` gains the « Anciens » count chip, absent when the count is zero

**GREEN**: `supabase/alumni-migration.sql` (idempotent `add column if not
exists`), `PlayerStatus` + two fields on `Player` in `src/types.ts`, `status` and
`left_at` in the `updatePlayer` patch type, the selector changes above, the
`muted` prop on `Avatar.tsx`, and the modal toggle in `Players.tsx`.

The `muted` avatar: `filter: grayscale(1)` plus an opacity drop on the photo; the
initials fallback **swaps** its team tint for neutral grey (`#8E889C`) rather
than being filtered, because greyscaling a coloured tint gives mud.

**MUTATE**: `npx stryker run --mutate src/lib/joueurs.ts`

**KILL MUTANTS**: Expect survivors around the sort comparator (alumni-last vs.
alumni-first will not be caught by a test whose fixture has alumni already at the
bottom by rating) and the zero-count chip guard. Both are worth killing.

**REFACTOR**: Assess whether `departureLabel` belongs in `format.ts` next to the
other French date helpers rather than in `joueurs.ts`.

**Done when**: Thibault can archive and un-archive a player from the annuaire,
the archived row is greyed with a B&W avatar behind the « Anciens » chip, and the
migration has been run against Supabase.

### Slice 2: Alumni drop out of the all-time ladder into « Anciens »

**Status**: ✅ Implemented and green (2026-08-14) — RED/GREEN/MUTATE(5/5 killed)/REFACTOR-assessed all done. Not yet visually verified in a browser (no Supabase credentials / node_modules in this environment) — verified via unit tests + typecheck only.

**Value**: The actual problem. The classement stops being filled by people who
will never play again, and ranks 1..N mean "the current field".

**Path**: Classement, scope « Depuis toujours » → the replay runs unchanged over
every match including alumni matches → the ranked rows are split into those who
hold a rank and les anciens → the table renders contiguous ranks 1..N → an
« Anciens » section renders below it → the podium, the gaps pill, the
progressions card and the dashboard « Top joueurs » all read the ranked half.

Skipped intentionally: season scopes still rank alumni (slice 3).

**Required implementation skills**: `tdd`, `testing`, `mutation-testing`,
`refactoring`.

**Acceptance criteria**:
- Ranks in the all-time ladder are contiguous from 1 with no holes where an alumnus used to sit
- An alumnus with matches in scope appears under « Anciens » with their frozen rating, their departure line, and no rank number
- An alumnus with no matches in scope appears nowhere on the page
- The « Anciens » section does not render when nobody in scope has left
- An alumnus never appears in the podium, the tightest-gaps pill, or the top progressions
- The dashboard « Top joueurs » shows five active players
- Every active player's rating is byte-identical to what it was before this slice

**RED**: New `src/lib/alumni.test.ts`.
- `splitLadder(rows, players)` returns `{ ranked, anciens }`; a ladder of three actives and one alumnus in second place yields ranked ranks 1, 2, 3 — the assertion that catches the renumbering bug
- `anciens` keeps rating order among themselves
- an alumnus whose rating row is absent (no matches in scope) appears in neither half
- rows matched by name only (pre-registry matches, `rankRatings` falls back to `teamByName`) resolve to the right status
- the ratings themselves are untouched: the same fixture through `replayRatings` before and after produces identical ratings for the active players — the invariant test, and the one worth writing first

**GREEN**: `src/lib/alumni.ts` exporting `splitLadder`. `rankRatings` is **not**
modified — it keeps ranking everyone, which is what keeps this a display concern.
`Ratings.tsx` calls `splitLadder` once and feeds `ranked` to `podium`,
`tightestGaps` and `topProgressions` (no signature changes to `classement.ts`),
renders the Anciens section, and `TopPlayers.tsx` slices `ranked`.

**MUTATE**: `npx stryker run --mutate src/lib/alumni.ts`

**KILL MUTANTS**: The rank arithmetic (`i + 1` vs `i`) and the sort stability are
the two places a survivor means a real bug. Off-by-one in renumbering is exactly
the mutant this slice exists to catch.

**REFACTOR**: `Ratings.tsx` is already 21K. If the Anciens section pushes it
further, assess extracting the ladder table into its own component — but only if
the extraction is clean, not to hit a line count.

**Done when**: the all-time classement ranks active players contiguously, alumni
sit below in their own section, and no active player's rating moved.

### Slice 3: Season ladders apply the closure rule

**Status**: ✅ Implemented and green (2026-08-15) — RED/GREEN/MUTATE(6/6 killed)/REFACTOR-assessed all done. `splitLadder` gained the optional `season` param and Ratings.tsx's Slice-2 all-time-only guard was removed (splitLadder now handles both scopes uniformly). Not visually verified in a browser (same environment limitation as Slice 2).

**Value**: A new season starts clean — nobody who has left is expected to be
ranked in it — while a season won fairly before someone's departure keeps its
champion.

**Path**: Classement, a season scope → the season replay runs unchanged → the
ranked/anciens split now takes the season into account → the champion, the season
banner and the archive line all read the ranked half.

**The rule**, one comparison:

```
ranksInSeason = status === 'active' || (left_at !== null && left_at >= season.end)
```

You rank in a season iff you were still with the company when it closed. A
`left_at` of null on an alumnus means "left long ago" — excluded everywhere.

**Required implementation skills**: `tdd`, `testing`, `mutation-testing`,
`refactoring`.

**Acceptance criteria**:
- An alumnus is not ranked in any season starting after their departure
- An alumnus who left mid-season is not ranked in that season, and appears under « Anciens » for it
- An alumnus who left after a season closed keeps their rank and their title in that season
- An alumnus with a null departure date is ranked in no season
- The season champion and the banner leader are always players who hold a rank
- When every ranked player in a season has left, the banner says so in its own words rather than blaming the provisional gate

**RED**: `src/lib/alumni.test.ts` and `src/lib/seasons.test.ts`.
- the three boundary cases of the rule, written as three explicit tests: departure before `season.start`, inside the window, and on or after `season.end`
- the exact boundary: `left_at === season.end` ranks (they were there for the whole window; the season ends exclusive)
- `seasonChampion` over a ladder whose leader is an alumnus returns the highest ranked active player
- `seasonBannerState` returns a distinct state when the only eligible players have all left — currently `nochamp`, whose copy claims nobody reached the games gate, which would be a lie

**GREEN**: `splitLadder` gains an optional `season: Season | null` parameter
implementing the comparison. `seasonChampion` and the banner leader consume
`ranked`. A new banner copy variant in `seasons.ts:seasonArchiveLine` /
`seasonBannerState` for the all-departed case.

**MUTATE**: `npx stryker run --mutate src/lib/alumni.ts,src/lib/seasons.ts`

**KILL MUTANTS**: The `>=` in the closure rule is the mutant that matters — `>`
versus `>=` is the difference between keeping and losing a title for anyone who
left on the exact day a season closed. The boundary test above exists to kill it.

**REFACTOR**: Assess whether the rule reads better as a named predicate
(`ranksInSeason(player, season)`) exported for the banner to reuse, rather than
inlined in the split.

**Done when**: a season that closed before a departure still crowns its champion,
and every season after it excludes them.

### Slice 4: A partie with an ancien is forced « non classée »

**Value**: A departed colleague can drop by and play without reshuffling a live
season, and without their own rating moving in a ladder they no longer appear in.

**Path**: Nouvelle Partie → the picker lists active players only → a « Voir les
anciens » toggle reveals alumni, muted with B&W avatars → selecting one forces
the enjeu to « non classée », locks the toggle, and explains why → the tournament
is created with `unranked: true` → `ratedMatches` excludes it from the replay,
as it already does for every unranked tournament.

**Required implementation skills**: `tdd`, `testing`, `mutation-testing`,
`refactoring`.

**Acceptance criteria**:
- The picker hides alumni by default and reveals them behind a toggle
- Selecting an alumnus forces the partie « non classée » and locks the enjeu control
- The note explains that an ancien is playing, distinctly from the doubles reason
- Deselecting the last alumnus releases the lock and restores the previous enjeu choice
- A partie created with an alumnus moves nobody's Elo

**RED**: `src/lib/nouvellePartie.test.ts`.
- `noteEnjeu` gains its third reason and returns the ancien wording when an ancien is playing, taking precedence over the doubles wording when both are true
- a selector deciding the forced-unranked state from the selected ids and the registry — this is the branch that must not live inside `NouvellePartie.tsx`
- releasing the lock restores the prior choice rather than defaulting to classée

**GREEN**: `unrankedEffectif` at `NouvellePartie.tsx:175` becomes
`isDouble || unranked || hasAncien`, driven by the tested selector. The picker
gains the toggle. No change to `rating.ts` — `ratedMatches` already excludes
unranked tournaments (`rating.ts:273`).

**MUTATE**: `npx stryker run --mutate src/lib/nouvellePartie.ts`

**KILL MUTANTS**: The precedence between the doubles and ancien reasons, and the
restore-previous-choice behaviour, are both easy to leave untested.

**REFACTOR**: `NouvellePartie.tsx` is 35K and the largest component in the app.
Do not grow it further than the toggle requires; assess whether the picker list
is worth extracting.

**Done when**: an ancien can be picked for a partie, the partie is unranked, and
no rating moves.

## Pre-PR quality gate

1. Mutation testing — `mutation-testing` skill, scoped with `--mutate` to the slice's modules
2. Refactoring assessment — `refactoring` skill
3. `npm run build` (runs `tsc -b`) and `npm test` pass
4. Thibault verifies the visuals themself — no automated browser check

## Deliberately not built

- **The « Les Anciens » hall-of-fame page.** Spec §Later. The archive is the
  useful half; the page is the half people will talk about, and it needs no new
  data — `left_at`, the replay and `rating_events` already carry all of it.
- **Skipping alumni in Slack invitations.** The spec calls for it, but
  `slack_user_id` is not in the DB schema yet — `db.ts:26` says so and
  `updatePlayer` strips the field before writing. There is nothing to filter on
  until that column lands. Revisit with the Slack auth work.
- **Auto-inactivity.** No rule archives anyone for not playing. Departure is a
  human fact, entered by a human.
- **Filtering alumni out of the Stats page.** Explicitly rejected — it would
  quietly rewrite the app's memory of who did what.
- **Rating decay for alumni.** Their rating freezes on their last match.
  `decayRd` already inflates RD, which is the only decay that means anything here
  and gives a returning player faster movement for free.
